# LeadFlow — Product Brief

## What is it?
LeadFlow is a CRM platform for managing contacts, tracking sales opportunities, and organizing customer interactions in one place.

## Who is it for?
Freelancers, independent professionals, and small businesses that need a practical way to manage customer relationships and sales pipelines.

## How should it feel?
Professional, trustworthy, focused, and intuitive. The interface should feel like a polished productivity tool: clean, fast, accessible, and easy to navigate.
